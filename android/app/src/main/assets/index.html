AURA

Connect to your assistant

AURA runs on your own computer. This app talks to it over your local wifi - nothing is uploaded, and no internet connection is needed.

Where do I find that address?
  • Start AURA on your computer (double-click AURA.exe).
  • Its window prints a line: phone (same wifi): http://192.168.x.x:8765.
  • Type those numbers here, then press Connect.
  • If nothing appears, launch AURA with --lan to allow phone access.
